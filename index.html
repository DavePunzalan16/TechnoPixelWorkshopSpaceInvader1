HOW TO PLAY

⌨️ KEYBOARD

  • A / ← Move Left
  • D / → Move Right
  • SPACE Shoot
  • P Pause / Resume
  • F Toggle Fullscreen
  • ENTER Start / Restart

📱 MOBILE

  • 🕹️ Analog Stick — Move
  • 🔥 FIRE Button — Shoot
  • ⏸️ PAUSE Button — Pause

🎯 OBJECTIVE

  1. Press Start or ENTER to begin.
  2. Destroy invaders before they reach the ground.
  3. Dodge enemy projectiles.
  4. Collect power-ups to upgrade your blaster.
  5. Earn points across 5 increasingly harder waves.
  6. Survive all 5 waves to win!

📡 WAVE PROGRESSION

WAVE 1

Formation of 30 invaders. Slow speed. 2 side invaders max.

WAVE 2

Faster movement. More frequent enemy fire. 3 side invaders.

WAVE 3

Larger formation. Enemy shots speed up. 4 side invaders.

WAVE 4

Enemies descend faster. Frequent multi-shot from enemies.

WAVE 5 ⚡

Final assault. Maximum speed. Survive to save the Pixelverse!

ENEMY TYPES

FORMATION INVADERS

  • BLUE INVADER 10 PTS Most common. Slow and predictable.
  • RED INVADER 20 PTS Fires more frequently. Watch out!
  • GREEN INVADER 50 PTS Rare and tough. High value target.
  • PINK INVADER 100 PTS Extremely rare. Maximum points!

SIDE INVADERS

These rogue invaders sweep across the screen from either side. Shoot them for bonus points!

  • BLUE RAIDER 10 PTS Swoops across at moderate speed.
  • RED RAIDER 20 PTS Faster than the blue raider.
  • GREEN RAIDER 50 PTS Rare and speedy. High reward!
  • PINK RAIDER 100 PTS Extremely rare side invader. Maximum bonus!

POWER-UPS

Destroy enemies for a 20% chance to drop a power-up. Fly into it to collect!

  • EXTRA LIFE Restores 1 life (max 5). sheesh
  • SHIELD Absorbs 1 hit. Shown as a glowing ring. sheesh
  • MULTISHOT Fires 3 bullets in a spread for 8 seconds. sheesh
  • BIG BULLET Large powerful shots for 8 seconds. sheesh
  • RAPID SHOT Doubles fire rate for 8 seconds. sheesh

THE STORY

The Pixelverse is under attack. A breach in the Neon Rift has unleashed waves of invaders into the digital frontier. Pilot the TechNoPixel spaceship, defend the grid, collect powerful upgrades, and push back the invasion. Each wave brings faster enemies and greater danger. Only a true arcade pilot can survive the final assault.
